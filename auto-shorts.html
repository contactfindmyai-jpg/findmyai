auto-shorts

A personal tool that turns long gaming videos into YouTube Shorts and uploads them to its operator's own YouTube channel.

Built with YouTube API Services. auto-shorts uses the YouTube Data API (method videos.insert only) to upload Shorts, with their title, description and tags, to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service. By using auto-shorts you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

What it does

1. Selects the strongest moments of a long-form gaming video by LaSalle, who gave written permission for this use.
2. Reframes each moment to vertical 9:16 and adds subtitles.
3. Uploads each Short to the operator's channel. Every Short credits LaSalle and links to his original full video.

Who uses it

auto-shorts is an internal tool. It runs only on the operator's own computer and is used only by its operator, Kélyan Edmond. It is not distributed, sold or offered to other users.

Permissions

auto-shorts requests only the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It collects no data from YouTube or from other people. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings.
